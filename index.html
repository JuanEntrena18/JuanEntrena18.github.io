features --list

Everything a stream process needs to stay alive.

Every module talks over Socket.IO in real time, so what happens in chat shows up on screen instantly.

Overlays

57 standalone overlays

Pure HTML/CSS/JS, no framework required. Eight themed styles including Subnautica 2, Fortnite, Retro 8-bit, Win95, and RetroWave — start screens, gameplay, BRB, and farewell states included.

Overlays

Vertical & animated

12 vertical overlays (1080×1920) for mobile and dual-format streaming, plus animated alerts for follows, subs, raids, cheers, and redemptions.

Overlays

Live in-app preview

An interactive preview with dynamic scaling and simulated Demo Mode data, so you can design overlays without going live.

Chat

Real-time Twitch IRC

Live chat relayed to overlays instantly, with TTS voice selection, speed and filters, and auto-greetings for new chatters.

Chat

Giveaways & predictions

Weighted-ticket giveaways with an animated wheel, plus Twitch Predictions with automatic resolution.

Chat

Custom commands

Aliases and cooldowns for every command, so your chat runs itself while you focus on the game.

Analytics

KPI dashboard

Historical stats across 7d, 30d, 90d and all-time, plus Twitch Achievement tracking for Affiliate and Partner milestones.

Analytics

Stream HUD & scoreboard

Live viewer, follower and sub counts, a Fighter Overlay and scoreboard for tournaments, and a Fortnite stats overlay for kills, wins and K/D.

Security

Encrypted & rate-limited

OAuth tokens encrypted with AES-256-GCM, local API auth on every request, anti-bot protection, and Zod-validated input on all routes.
